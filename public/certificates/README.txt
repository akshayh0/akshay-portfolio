Certificates live here.

For each certificate you have:
  1. A thumbnail image, e.g.  my-cert.jpg   (screenshot or export of page 1)
  2. Optionally the original PDF, e.g.  my-cert.pdf

Then add an entry in src/data/certificates.js (title, issuer, date, type,
category, file, pdf). It shows up automatically in the Certifications gallery.
